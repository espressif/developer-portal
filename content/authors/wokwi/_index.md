---
title: "Wokwi"
---

[Wokwi](https://wokwi.com) is an online simulator for embedded systems, IoT and electronics. You can use it to simulate
various microcontrollers, including Espressif's ESP32, ESP32-C3, ESP32-S3, ESP32-H2 and ESP32-S31.

Wokwi allows you to begin writing your firmware without needing the hardware in your hands. With ESP-IDF integration and
an official VS Code extension, you can write, build, simulate and test all within the same window.
